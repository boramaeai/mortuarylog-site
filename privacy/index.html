Privacy Policy — MortuaryLog

Last updated: 5 September 2026

MortuaryLog is a case log for funeral service and embalmer apprentices in the United States. This policy describes what the app does with information. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version. Everything you log stays on your phone. We never receive it. We have no server that could hold it, no account to sign in to, and no analytics or advertising code in the app at all.

What stays on your device

The app stores this on your phone, in a database only the app can open:

  • Details about the people you served — name, address, age, sex, cause of death, whether an autopsy or organ donation took place, and anything else your state board's form asks for. This is information about someone else, and some of it is health information.
  • Your own details — your name, apprenticeship or registration number, and your supervisor's name and licence number.
  • Your work — which cases you logged, what you did on each one, hours worked per week, and any requirement you changed or added yourself.
  • Reports you prepared — which form, on what date, and which version of our rule data produced it.

We never receive any of it. There is no account, no server of ours holds it, and nothing is synchronised to us. If you delete the app, our copy is gone — there never was one.

It is not encrypted. The database sits inside the app's private storage, which the operating system keeps other apps out of, but we do not add encryption of our own. A backup file you export is plain text and is not encrypted either — the app says so on screen when you make one. Treat both the way you would treat a paper case report.

What leaves your device

Three things.

1. Rule and form updates. When you open the app, it asks our update service (u.expo.dev, operated by Expo) whether newer requirement data is available. That request carries your platform, the app's runtime version, and the identifier of the data you already have. It carries nothing about you and nothing about any case. We use it for one purpose: getting corrected state requirements onto your phone when a board changes its rules. It is not optional, because an apprentice relying on out-of-date requirements can miss a licence deadline.

2. Your phone's own backup, if you have it switched on. Android's Auto Backup and Apple's iCloud Backup copy an app's private files into your Google or Apple account. This app does not turn that on, cannot see it, and cannot read it — it is a feature of your phone, controlled in your phone's settings, and it goes to your account and not to us. We do not switch it off, because it is often what puts your records back on a replacement phone. Two things to know: it only restores when the app is installed, so it will not undo a case you deleted; and if you would rather your case data never went to Google or Apple, turn backup off for this app in your phone's settings and export your own file instead.

3. Anything you choose to send. When you prepare a form, export your details, or make a backup, the app hands the file to your phone's own share sheet or download folder. Where it goes next is your choice and is governed by whatever app you send it to. If you use the "my board's form is different" button, the app shows you the exact text before you send it — that text contains the state, the form, and what you typed, and no case details and nothing about any decedent.

What we do not do

  • We do not have user accounts, and you never sign in.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind. There are none in the app.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your information, because we do not have it.
  • We do not submit anything to your licensing board. The app prepares paperwork; filing it is yours to do.

Deleting your information

Settings has an "erase everything" option. It really erases — including the revision history the app otherwise keeps on purpose, so that a phone you sell or hand on does not carry a decedent's details. It cannot be undone, and the app asks you twice.

There is nothing for us to delete on our side, because we never held it.

Children

MortuaryLog is a professional tool for licensed apprentices. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect anything from anyone.

About the information you enter concerning other people

The people whose details you record are not our users, and they cannot ask us anything, because we never see their information. You are recording it to meet a requirement your state board places on you, and you hold it under whatever obligations your employer and your board place on you. The app is designed around that: it keeps that information on your device, it marks the most sensitive fields as staying on the device, and it warns you that an exported backup is not encrypted.

Changes to this policy

If this changes, the date at the top changes and the current version is always at this address. If a change ever means information starts leaving your device, we will say so plainly, in the app, before it happens.
