Where the requirements come from

MortuaryLog is not a government app. It is an independent app published by Studio82. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of any government agency, state licensing board, or professional association, and it does not represent any of them. It cannot submit anything to a licensing authority on your behalf.

Everything the app tells you about a state's apprenticeship requirements is summarized from the published sources listed below, and the app names the source for each requirement on screen. Summaries can be wrong or out of date — confirm anything that matters with your own licensing authority.
