MortuaryLog

A case log for funeral service and embalmer apprentices in the United States. It holds the licensing requirements for all fifty states, summarized from each state's own published regulations, and keeps the record your licensing authority asks you to keep.

MortuaryLog is not a government app. It is an independent app published by Studio82. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for any government agency, state licensing board or professional association, and it does not represent any of them. It cannot submit anything to a licensing authority for you.

Every requirement names the source it was read from, and you can open that source in the app. Some states are read from a published reprint of the regulation rather than the state's own site, and a reprint can lag. The full list is at Where the requirements come from.

Everything you log stays on your phone.
